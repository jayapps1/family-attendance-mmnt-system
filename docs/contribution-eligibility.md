# Contribution management completion report

1. **Files changed for this task:** new `services/contribution_eligibility.py`, `ui/contributions/contribution_periods.py`, `ui/contributions/recent_payments.py`, and `tests/test_contribution_eligibility.py`; updated `services/simple_contribution.py`, `services/contribution_service.py`, `services/report_service.py`, `repositories/contribution_repository.py`, contribution dashboard/register/details/period form/payment form/payment history, family member form/profile, shared `ui/table_style.py` and `ui/components.py`, finance/report/affiliation/UI tests, README and contribution documentation. Other working-tree changes belong to earlier audit and packaging work.
2. **Eligibility:** the central service helper calculates age from DOB at the date of evaluation. Only living members aged 23 or older qualify. The exact 23rd birthday qualifies. Existing archived-member protection remains; unknown living status requires review. Assignment and both payment entry paths enforce the same rule under their transaction locks.
3. **Deceased members:** cannot receive new obligations or payments. They are excluded from current eligible, expected, unpaid and outstanding totals, including when they already have an obligation.
4. **Missing DOB:** produces a review-required state, no prospective debt, disabled payment and an Edit Member action. Saving a DOB refreshes eligibility and the payment preview.
5. **History:** obligations and payment records are retained. A deceased member with GHS 60 due and GHS 30 paid keeps the original partial-payment history, but has zero current outstanding. Closed or prior-year periods use recorded obligations and retain their historical balances. Reversals retain the original transaction and require a reason; there is no payment-delete action. Historical eligibility snapshots are not reconstructed.
6. **Totals:** current expected/outstanding and paid/partial/unpaid counts use eligible members. Annual previews include eligible members who have not yet paid without inserting obligations. Collected cash includes valid payments retained from members who are now ineligible; therefore collected plus outstanding need not equal current expected. Reversed payments are excluded from collection totals. Separate under-23, deceased and DOB-review cards explain exclusions.
7. **Contribution Periods:** dedicated page with active-period details, actual statistics, period-history table, view/edit/activate/close/new actions and an empty state. Closed periods cannot be edited. Amount/type/year changes are blocked after obligations exist to preserve financial records.
8. **Recent Payments:** dedicated page with actual today/month/current-period totals, member/receipt, period, method, status and date filters, receipt/actor details, and retained reversal records. Empty and filtered-empty states are supported. Summary cards describe their independent scope; table filters do not silently alter them.
9. **Register and profile:** the register defaults to Eligible and provides eligibility/status filters, age, eligibility and appropriate actions. Profiles display eligibility separately from payment status and retain accessible installment history. Ineligible members cannot pay through either screen.
10. **Alignment and navigation:** shared Back/Home navigation, matching header/cell anchors, right-aligned money, centered dates/status fields, wider eligibility labels, horizontal table scrolling and scrollable dashboard content. Existing navy/teal/gold styling is retained.
11. **Schema:** no model or database schema change, no stored age field and no migration required. Existing obligation amounts and payment records are not rewritten.
12. **Automated tests:** birthday boundaries, leap-day age, deceased precedence, missing DOB, unknown living status, archived records, the 10-member eligibility scenario, death after partial payment, stale eligibility at save, three installments, reversal retention, report totals, and real Tkinter edit/payment/navigation flows.
